Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Stages:
  • Stage 1: ≥ 90 (Normal or high GFR)
  • Stage 2: 60 - 89 (Mildly decreased GFR)
  • Stage 3a: 45 - 59 (Mild-moderately decreased GFR)
  • Stage 3b: 30 - 44 (Moderate-severely decreased GFR)
  • Stage 4: 15 - 29 (Severely decreased GFR)
  • Stage 5: < 15 (Kidney Failure)

Note: This calculator uses the original MDRD formula, which includes a race-based variable. Modern guidelines often recommend the CKD-EPI 2021 equation, which omits race.
